Use case: illustration-story. A wide 3:2 explanatory watercolor illustration for a Chinese illustrated city guide article on the Summer Palace in Beijing. One calm continuous elevated view from the waters of Kunming Lake toward the southern front of Longevity Hill. Composition must make the three spatial layers immediately legible even on a mobile screen: ample unbroken blue-green lake in foreground (bottom 40%); along the northern lakeshore at the foot of the hill a distinctly long, narrow traditional covered corridor with intermittent open sides, parallel to shoreline; in middle distance terraced ochre and vermilion palace halls ascending the green hill to the recognizable tall octagonal multi-eaved Tower of Buddhist Incense. Paint clearly distinguishable water, horizontal corridor and climbing central buildings. Muted jade, mineral blue, ochre tiles, and aged ivory, refined East Asian travel-book watercolor with delicate pencil line and subtle paper grain. Bright soft spring light. Simplified spatial impression, not a navigation map. No islands or bridges in this framing, no text, no letters, no arrows, no panels, no border, no watermark.
